# Degen Desk — Marketing Partner Program

**A recurring-revenue partnership for operators who bring creators to Degen Desk.**

---

## What is Degen Desk?

Degen Desk is an AI-powered research assistant for Solana meme coin traders. Subscribers get unlimited access to live token data, trending coins, KOL tracking, and chart analysis through a chat-first interface — on web and iOS. Pro subscriptions are $14.99/mo on web and $17.99/mo on iOS, billed monthly or annually.

We already pay a generous referral commission (15%) to individual creators who bring us paying users. The **Marketing Partner Program** is a second layer on top of that — for operators who sign up and manage multiple creators at once.

---

## How the partnership works

You recruit creators (KOLs, trader Twitter accounts, Discord community leads, Telegram admins). Each creator gets their own referral code and earns their own 15% cut on every subscription they drive.

**As the marketing partner, you earn a separate commission on every paying subscription driven by any creator in your stable — layered on top of the creator's own cut. You do not reduce the creator's earnings.**

- You are paid from Degen Desk's share, not from the creator's share
- Creators have no incentive to work around you — their cut is the same either way
- Tracking is fully automatic once an admin links a creator to you

---

## Your commission tiers

Partner commission scales with the number of **active creators** in your roster each month. An active creator is one who drove at least one paying subscription that month.

| Tier | Active creators this month | Your rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Standard** | Fewer than 15 | **10%** |
| **Elite** | 15 or more | **12%** |

Tiers recalculate in real time — the moment your 15th creator activates in a calendar month, every new commission that month is paid at 12%. Counts reset at the start of each month.

**Your 10–12% is layered on top of the creator's own 15% cut.** Neither cut comes out of the other.

## Economics at a glance

On a $14.99/month web Pro subscription:

| Cut | Amount per subscriber per month |
| --- | --- |
| Creator (15%) | $2.25 |
| Partner — Standard (10%) | $1.50 |
| Partner — Elite (12%) | $1.80 |

**Worked example — you run a stable of 20 active creators, each of whom has brought in 10 paying monthly subscribers:**

- 20 creators × 10 subscribers = **200 paying subscribers under your umbrella**
- Monthly revenue under your umbrella: 200 × $14.99 = **$2,998 GMV/month**
- You're Elite tier → 12% commission
- **Your monthly commission: 200 × $1.80 = $360/month**

And that recurs every month as long as those users stay subscribed. If all 200 renew for a year, that's **$4,320/year in passive recurring income** from this one cohort.

Scale that to 40 creators × 15 subs = 600 paying users → **$1,080/month** at Elite tier.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I earn on subscriptions the creator drove before joining my stable?**
No — only commissions from subscriptions dated after the creator is linked to you are credited to your partner balance. Past commissions remain with the creator only.

**What if a creator leaves my stable?**
You stop earning on any new subscriptions they drive. You keep the commissions you've already earned (they're already in your balance).

**What if a subscriber cancels?**
You earn only on actual payments. If a subscriber cancels, no more commissions are generated for that user, but everything earned to date is yours.

**What about iOS subscribers?**
iOS subscriptions are priced higher ($17.99/month) to offset Apple's 30% in-app purchase fee in year 1 (15% after that). Your commission is calculated on the customer-facing price — so a 10% partner cut on a $17.99 iOS sub is $1.80/month, the same as a 12% Elite cut on a $14.99 web sub.

**What about chargebacks / refunds?**
We reserve the right to claw back commissions on refunded payments, but we don't claw back commissions that are older than 30 days even if disputed.

**Is there a minimum commitment?**
No. Either side can end the partnership at any time with 30 days' notice. Commissions in your account at termination are paid out normally.

**When do I get paid?**
Whenever you want, as long as your balance is ≥ $10. Request from your dashboard and we'll process within 1–3 business days.
