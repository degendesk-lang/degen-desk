Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time and keep Pro access until the end of your current billing period. On iOS, cancel from your Apple ID subscription settings. On web, cancel from the billing portal linked in your account.
What happens after my 3-day free trial?
The 3-day free trial is available on the yearly plan when subscribing through the iOS app. If you don't cancel before the trial ends, your Apple ID is charged for the full year and your Pro access continues. Cancel any time during the trial at no cost.
What's the difference between the standard and upgraded models?
Free users get the standard model (GPT-4o-mini), which handles most crypto and meme coin questions well. Pro users get the upgraded model (GPT-4o), which is better at nuanced analysis, complex multi-step reasoning, and on-chain research questions — especially useful for chart reading, wallet tracking on Solscan, risk assessment, and narrative deep dives.
Is this financial advice?
No. Degen Desk is an educational and research tool. Nothing in the app is financial, investment, or trading advice. Crypto and meme coin trading is extremely high risk and you can lose everything you put in. Always do your own research and never invest more than you can afford to lose.
Can I switch between monthly and yearly plans?
Yes. On iOS, switch plans from your Apple ID subscription settings. On web, you can upgrade or downgrade at any time from the billing portal — changes take effect at your next renewal.
Do you offer refunds?
Subscriptions purchased through the iOS app are subject to Apple's refund policy — request a refund directly from Apple via reportaproblem.apple.com. Web subscriptions purchased through Stripe are eligible for a refund within 7 days of purchase if you haven't used your Pro access. Contact support@degendesk.xyz for help.
