## Seller name (Apple Developer enrollment)

**Launch phase (April 2026 – September 2026):**
Legal name of the developer as enrolled in the Apple Developer Program Individual
track. This is set during Apple Developer enrollment and cannot be changed while
on an Individual account. It will appear publicly on the App Store listing under
the "Seller" field. Keep this the only place the legal name appears.

**Post-LLC phase (September 17, 2026 onward):**
`Degen Desk LLC` (or final LLC name). Use App Store Connect's "Transfer App"
feature to move the app from the Individual developer account to the LLC's
Organization developer account. Seller name on the public listing updates
automatically after transfer.
